CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The compiler successfully auto-vectorizes simple element-wise loops (magnitude,
direction) but fails on Gaussian and Sobel due to boundary checks. These two
stages account for ~70% of execution time (Gaussian: 42%, Sobel: 28%).

Manual RVV intrinsics for Gaussian and Sobel are justified by:
  1. Profiling data shows ~70% hotspot concentration
  2. Compiler cannot auto-vectorize due to control flow
  3. RVV VLA design allows one binary to work at all VLEN values

This is Amdahl's Law in practice: optimizing the 70% hotspot yields a ~9-10x
total pipeline speedup; optimizing the remaining 30% (direction: 4.3%) would
add less than 5% improvement to overall runtime.
